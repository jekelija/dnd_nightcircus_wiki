# Little Rock

The Night Circus's next stop, deduced from the fragment of granite folded into [[granite-note|the note]] left for the party in the empty circus field.

Reaching it from Springfield takes two three-hour trains. [[cornelius-pagewick|Dean Cornelius Pagewick]] funded the tickets on the understanding that the trip is an Enchanted Archaeology field trip to view the Cave Poems in the Granite — generously enough that the party rides first class.

The party has written to [[jake|Jake]] to meet them there.

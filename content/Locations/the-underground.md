# The Underground

A sprawling stone maze beneath the world, reached by tearing a slip of paper marked "ROBERT" that [[riley-o-shaughnessy|Riley O'Shaughnessy]] gave the party at the Night Circus. There was no door behind them when they arrived, and no way out until they found one.

## Layout

The maze is built almost entirely of identical rooms: roughly fifty feet square, hewn from rock, with natural stone formations jutting from the walls and doors set into the cardinal walls. There is no light of its own — the party navigated the first stretch entirely by [[poppy-skycleaver|Poppy]]'s glow.

The geometry does not hold still. One room span on its axis and dumped the party out facing a different exit; another threw [[mel|Mel]] bodily into the Astral Plane, and she had to be dragged back through a wall. Sound behaves badly too: one room swallows all noise completely, and the moaning the party tracked for a dozen rooms would fall silent whenever they got close.

The only rooms that look like anything else are the ones built to hold something — the chasm, the cup room, the well, and the waiting room at the end.

## Hazards

Traps are everywhere and mostly unmarked: runes and glyphs that discharge on contact, rusted iron spikes that swing down from the ceiling, interstitial walls that close in, and floors that crumble underfoot. The wildlife is worse — bats, a swarm of giant rats, a minor Chaos Elemental, a massive stone-and-earth Golem, and an enormous pulsating green ooze.

Deep in the maze, the floor of one room has caved in almost entirely, leaving five-foot ledges on either side of a jagged pit thirty to forty feet deep. Two sets of empty manacles hang from iron rings bolted into the far wall.

## Puzzles

Three of the maze's rooms are built as deliberate tests, and all three are cruel:

- **The Cup Room.** Iron grates depicting people dying and screaming slam over every exit. Six pedestals; [[robert-frost|Robert Frost]] laid out on one, ten numbered drink containers on another. Grouping the cups wrong raises boiling water through the floor. They must be sorted by **scent**, not by number.
- **The Well.** A well full of water rises from the floor, with five small mugs around its rim, five large mugs sealed in a glass case, and a key sealed in a glass box. A 3:30 countdown starts the moment a cup is lifted. The party solved it — and the key vanished from their hand anyway.
- **The Waiting Room.** Chairs along the walls and an inactive portal opposite. A huge stone platter lies smashed across the floor beside a recessed circle and a button, plaqued _"The first hint is free, after that you have to pay the piper."_ The hints cost blood; the portal is the way out.

## Who Was Down Here

The maze was holding people. [[robert-frost|Robert Frost]] was found on a pedestal, half his soul gone. [[nancy-ann-barbara|Nancy Ann Barbara]] was found on the floor past the chasm, hers gone entirely. The empty manacles at the pit suggest they were not the first.

[[mr-glass|Mr. Glass]] and the [[rotten-rabbit|Rotten Rabbit]] came at the party from deeper in, through a door that sealed itself behind them.

Laughter answers progress. It came when [[mel|Mel]] tore Riley's note, when the party's hand closed on the vanishing key, and again when Glass and the Rabbit arrived. Someone is watching.

## Party Interactions

### Session 3

The party arrived by tearing the "ROBERT" paper to escape [[marguerite-voss|Chief Voss]], and spent twenty-five rooms working through the maze: closing walls, a spike trap, a spinning room, the silent room, a Golem, a Chaos Elemental, and Mel's involuntary trip to the Astral Plane. They found a [[featherbone|Featherbone]], a throwing blade set in stone, and two short swords, and ended the session cornered in a dead end with rats piled against the door.

### Session 4

The party escaped the rats via [[wall-walk-spell|Wall Walk]], backtracked northeast on [[gulfoss|Gulfoss]]'s read of the trail, and fought their way across the collapsed-floor chasm. Past it they found [[nancy-ann-barbara|Nancy Ann Barbara]], soulless. They destroyed the green ooze, recovered the [[blinding-orb|Blinding Orb]], and solved the cup puzzle to free [[robert-frost|Robert Frost]]. They made camp and took a long rest.

### Session 5

The party solved the well puzzle, killed [[mr-glass|Mr. Glass]] and the [[rotten-rabbit|Rotten Rabbit]], reassembled the smashed stone platter in the waiting room, and stepped through the portal — out of the Underground at last, into the empty field where the Night Circus had been.

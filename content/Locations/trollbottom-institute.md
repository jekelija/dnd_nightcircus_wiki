# Trollbottom Institute

The **Trollbottom Institute** is a for-profit magical university operating on the respectable edge of legitimacy in early-1900s America. It advertises itself as _practical_, _forward-thinking_, and _career-minded_, catering to students who have talent but not pedigree. Its brick lecture halls hum with thaumaturgical experiments, industrial spellcraft, and applied enchantment—magic meant to **work**, not impress the elite academies back east. Trollbottom graduates become clerks of the arcane, municipal wardens, railway charmers, potion inspectors, and stage magicians: solid, employable work, if rarely glamorous. The Institute’s reputation is good enough to open doors, but rarely the _best_ ones, and everyone on campus knows it.

Student life centers around the **Sanctum Arcanum**, the sprawling student union that functions as commons, study hall, informal dueling forum, and social heart of the campus. Half-funded by tuition fees and half by questionable donor enchantments, the Sanctum is where rumors circulate faster than spellbooks and where students trade favors, notes, and contraband charms. Administrators tolerate it because it keeps students occupied and contained; students love it because it feels like the one place on campus that truly belongs to them. If something important is happening at Trollbottom—politically, magically, or disastrously—it’s being discussed in hushed voices beneath the Sanctum’s ever-flickering wards.

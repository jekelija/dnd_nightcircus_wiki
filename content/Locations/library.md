# Library at the Trollbottom Institute

The **Library at the Trollbottom Institute** is a bright star on an otherwise dreary campus. Ancient and massive, it contains tomes of knowledge that have been curated by the unmatched [[elowen-thricebound|Elowen Thricebound]]. Students make a peep here at their own risk, always in danger of being reprimanded by Elowen or her assistant [[bramwell-quill|Bramwell Quill]]. For those students that treat the books with respect, Elowen can be an ally, her knowledge of the stacks overflowing and inumerable.

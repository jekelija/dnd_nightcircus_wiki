# The Stone Lion

The **Stone Lion** is a bookstore in Springfield, curated by [[alfred-tennyson|Alfred Tennyson]], who lives in the apartment above it.

## Party Interactions

### Session 3

[[riley-o-shaughnessy|Riley O'Shaughnessy]] revealed that an explosive had been planted at the Stone Lion — everyone inside would be killed, including Tennyson. The party chose to save the Stone Lion over rescuing [[robert-frost|Robert Frost]], tearing a slip of paper to transport themselves directly there. They searched Tennyson's apartment, found the bomb, and defused it with 18 seconds on the clock. [[marguerite-voss|Marguerite Voss]], the Chief of Police, arrived shortly after to investigate.

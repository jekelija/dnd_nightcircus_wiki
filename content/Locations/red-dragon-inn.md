# Red Dragon Inn

The **Red Dragon Inn** is a seedy inn. The tavern is dimly lit, the panes of glass in the window are murky and dirty and only serve to illuminate the stains on the bartop. You never know who you might meet at the Red Dragon Inn; a vagabond on the run, a traveling wizard, a down-on-their-luck traveling merchant.

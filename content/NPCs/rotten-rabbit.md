# The Rotten Rabbit

## Role

The Rotten Rabbit is a famous performer — a genuine icon, and one [[candace-cheeks|Candace Cheeks]] has idolized since well before any of this started.

He appeared in the Underground fighting alongside [[mr-glass|Mr. Glass]]. Up close, the party could see that the rabbit mask had slipped a little.

Whatever he was on stage, in the maze he was there to kill them.

## Party Interactions

### Session 5

The Rotten Rabbit burst into the well room with [[mr-glass|Mr. Glass]] and fought the party. [[candace-cheeks|Cadence]] recognized him at once and explained to the others who he was, then found she could not bring herself to fight him — until his intention to kill them became unmistakable, at which point the shine came off her childhood hero for good. [[mel|Mel]] took him down entirely, which brought [[robert-frost|Frost]]'s Sanity up sharply. Mel looted a lute from the body and handed it to [[candace-cheeks|Cadence]]; [[robert-frost|Frost]] immediately smashed it. As the Rabbit fell, Mel's binding stopped glowing and her magic returned.

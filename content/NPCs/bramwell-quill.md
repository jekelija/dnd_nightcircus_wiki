# Bramwell Quill

## Role

Bramwell Quill is the Assistant Archivist of Circulating Collections at the [[trollbottom-institute|Trollbottom Institute]] library. He is rigid and particular — do not call him Bram. He is always looking to get students in trouble and desperately desires to become Head Librarian someday.

# Maribel Thorne — Carousel Guide

**Ancestry:** Faerie  
**Relation:** Sister of [[maelin-thorne|Maelin Thorne]]

## Vibe

Bright, breathless enthusiasm masking deep exhaustion.

## Role

Maribel welcomes riders and helps “match” guests with mounts, speaking as though the animals choose their riders.

She genuinely loves the carousel and believes it is harmless joy.

## Connections

- Protected (and manipulated) by [[maelin-thorne|Maelin Thorne]]
- Pities [[orin-bellweather|Orin Bellweather]] for seeing too much beauty
- Avoids [[mr-glass|Mr. Glass]] whenever possible

## Unease

She is noticeably drained after long nights.  
If pressed, she deflects questions about where the magic comes from.

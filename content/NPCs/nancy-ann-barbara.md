# Nancy Ann Barbara

## Family

Husband — [[jake|Jake]]

## Role

Nancy Ann Barbara is a sculptor, and the missing wife [[jake|Jake]] has been chasing across the country. She went to the Night Circus alone several weeks before the party's first visit and never came home. The ice statue standing in the [[the-ice-garden|Ice Garden]] is very nearly a perfect likeness of her, and as it melts it reforms into miniatures of her past work.

The party found her body in the Underground, alive but empty — [[mel|Mel]]'s examination determined that she has no soul. Her eyes stay open, her pupils do not dilate, and dried blood rings her wrists, but she has a pulse.

She is the same affliction [[robert-frost|Robert Frost]] was suffering, only much further along.

## Party Interactions

### Session 4

The party found her lying on the stone floor past the chasm. [[poppy-skycleaver|Poppy]] recognized her as the woman from the ice sculpture — Jake's missing wife. Mel diagnosed her as soulless. Poppy hoisted her over her shoulder and carried her onward.

### Session 5

Poppy carried her out of the maze and through the portal. When [[marguerite-voss|Marguerite Voss]] spotted a giant hauling a body across campus, [[mel|Mel]] disguised her as a sack of rice with an illusion. The party stashed her in Poppy's dorm and wrote to [[jake|Jake]] to tell him they had found her, asking him to meet them in [[little-rock|Little Rock]].

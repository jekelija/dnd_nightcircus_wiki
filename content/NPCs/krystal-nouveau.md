# Krystal Nouveau

## Role

Krystal Nouveau is a snooty French professor of transmutation at the [[trollbottom-institute|Trollbottom Institute]].

## Party Interactions

### Session 2

[[mel|Mel]] and [[gulfoss|Gulfoss]] visited Madame Nouveau. Gulfoss opened with a gambler's biscuit, causing her to hear phantom laughter. Despite the rocky start, she answered their questions about transmuting a living person into an ice sculpture: it's possible, but permanent, dangerous, and roughly a 50/50 shot even for her. She warned the result couldn't be reversed cleanly and said she had once seen it at a funeral — it would be easier to do on someone already dead. On her way out, she reminded Mel to study Animal Transmutations Volume III.

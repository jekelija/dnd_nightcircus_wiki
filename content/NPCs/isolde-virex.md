# Isolde Virex — Bearer of the Lanterns

**Ancestry:** Drakona  
**Associated Acts:** Lantern Procession, Ever-Burning Fire

## Role in the Circus

Isolde is the magician responsible for the Lantern Procession—a nightly ritual where floating lanterns drift through the grounds, responding to memories and emotions. She also oversees the fire magic used across multiple performances, though she rarely takes center stage herself.

## Public Face

Poised, distant, and reverent. Isolde loves performing with her fire.

## Party Interactions

They talked to her after her performance; she talked about the train being a hierarchy; she is only in the 3rd car, with janitors and other low workers. She has been a circus a long time but has her differences with Riley.

Her and [[candace-cheeks|Cheeks]] mixed their Drakona breaths (sand and fire) and made a glass butt plug that [[mel|Mel]] kept. She invited them to the Lantern Procession that night and pointed her to the tent for the fortune teller.

# Isolde Virex — Bearer of the Lanterns

**Ancestry:** Drakona  
**Associated Acts:** Lantern Procession, Ever-Burning Fire

## Role in the Circus

Isolde is the magician responsible for the Lantern Procession—a nightly ritual where floating lanterns drift through the grounds, responding to memories and emotions. She also oversees the fire magic used across multiple performances, though she rarely takes center stage herself.

## Public Face

Poised, distant, and reverent. Isolde treats magic as something that must be maintained, not enjoyed. She speaks of the lanterns as if they are listening.

## Private Truth

Isolde willingly gave up pieces of herself early on to stabilize the circus’s magic. She knows the lanterns are lighter when souls are fed into the system. She tells herself she is preserving beauty.

## Relationship to Riley

Professional respect layered with mutual suspicion. Riley relies on her craftsmanship but keeps her emotionally distant. He knows she remembers too much.

## Connections

- Aware of [[maelin-thorne|Maelin Thorne]]'s filtering role, but avoids confronting her
- Views [[elias-quill|Elias Quill]] as dangerous—not because he is wrong, but because he asks the wrong questions
- Resents [[mr-glass|Mr. Glass]] for how _efficiently_ he feeds the system

## How She Engages the Party

- Offers quiet explanations of the lanterns’ symbolism
- Asks reflective questions about memory and legacy
- May warn the party that some lights should not be followed

## Long-Term Payoff

Isolde can:

- Help the party track the flow of soul energy
- Sabotage the lanterns to cause chaos during the finale
- Refuse to act, choosing preservation over justice

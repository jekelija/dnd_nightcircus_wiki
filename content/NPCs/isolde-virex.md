# Isolde Virex

## Ancestry

Drakona

## Role

Isolde is the magician responsible for the Lantern Procession — a nightly ritual where floating lanterns drift through the grounds, responding to memories and emotions. She also oversees the fire magic used across multiple performances, though she rarely takes center stage herself. She has been with the circus for 20 years and sleeps in Car #3 of the train, sharing a compartment with [[seraphine-vale|Seraphine]]. More important staff ride in the rear cars.

## Vibe

Poised, distant, and reverent. Isolde loves performing with her fire.

## Party Interactions

### Session 2

The party spoke with Isolde after her performance at the [[the-fire-tent|Fire Tent]]. She described the train as a hierarchy — she rides in Car #3 with janitors and other low workers. She seems wistful about her time at the circus, though she has her differences with [[riley-o-shaughnessy|Riley]]. [[gulfoss|Gulfoss]] fed her a gambler's biscuit, causing her to hiccough rose petals, which warmed her up considerably. She and [[candace-cheeks|Cadence]] mixed their Drakona breaths — sand and fire — and created a [[glass-butt-plug|glass butt plug]] that [[mel|Mel]] kept. Isolde invited the party to see the Lantern Procession later that night and directed them to [[seraphine-vale|Seraphine]]'s tent.

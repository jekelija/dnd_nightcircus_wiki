# Kenton

## Role

Kenton is a fellow student at [[trollbottom-institute|Trollbottom]], and a one-time pal of [[poppy-skycleaver|Poppy's]]. She once tried to defend him, but in doing so, accidentally hurt him. Since then, Poppy has assumed that he hates her.

## Party Interactions

### Session 1

Poppy ran into Kenton briefly at the [[the-cloud-maze|Cloud Maze]]. He acknowledged her before jumping down and disappearing into the crowd.

### Session 2

The party encountered Kenton and his friend Frenton at the [[red-dragon-inn|Red Dragon Inn]]. [[gulfoss|Gulfoss]] played King's Bones against the pair and walked away with extra gold and a [[suspending-rod|Suspending Rod]].

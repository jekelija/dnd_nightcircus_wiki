# Kenton

## Summary

Kenton is a fellow student at [[trollbottom-institute|Trollbottom]], and a one-time pal of [[poppy-skycleaver|Poppy's]]. She once tried to defend him, but in doing so, accidentally hurt him. Since they, Poppy has assumed that he hates her.

## Party Interactions

They ran into him at the [[red-dragon-inn|Red Dragon Inn]] in Springfield. The party encountered him there alongside his friend Frenton. [[gulfoss-Gulfoss]] played King's Bones against Kenton and Frenton, walking away with extra gold and a suspending rod (36").

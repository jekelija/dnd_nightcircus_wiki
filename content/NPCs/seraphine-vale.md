# Seraphine Vale

## Ancestry

Drakona

## Role

Seraphine is the fortune teller at the Night Circus. She reads cards for guests from the [[the-fortune-teller|Fortune Teller's Tent]]. She and [[isolde-virex|Isolde Virex]] have been friends since school and joined the circus around the same time. They keep to themselves, away from the other performers, because they have "too many secrets." She sleeps in Car #3 of the train alongside Isolde.

## Party Interactions

### Session 3

The party visited Seraphine's tent. [[gulfoss|Gulfoss]] attempted to offer her a gambler's biscuit, but [[poppy-skycleaver|Poppy]] intercepted him. Seraphine laid out a seven-card reading for Poppy — the journey ahead appeared fraught with peril, with warnings about cups, bald men, opposing forces, and the possibility that one of the party may perish. The cards vanished as she finished, though the reading felt lackluster with Poppy's glow lighting up the tent. She warned the party they would not find what they are looking for, but might find what they are NOT looking for.

[[mel|Mel]] shared her orb with Seraphine, who heard a troubled spirit within it. She warned Mel not to take its words at face value and advised spending time with it during long rests for better results. When Mel peered into the orb afterward, she sensed the spirit may be sentient.

Cheeks asked if the cards could find a missing person. Seraphine read for Mel, who confessed her mother — a Mage of Primal Origin from the Wildborne clan — is not well. The reading revealed [[robert-frost|Robert]] carries a heavy burden and the party must reach him quickly, and that cups will be offered carrying both reward and penalty.

Seraphine mentioned her friendship with [[isolde-virex|Isolde]] and their shared history. When Poppy asked if many people go missing from the circus, Seraphine promptly kicked her out of the tent.

# Krispy Kripke

## Role

Krispy Kripke is a smithery professor at the [[trollbottom-institute|Trollbottom Institute]], originally from Florida. He lost his legs in a battle when he was much younger and now uses an arcane combat wheelchair constructed with the help of [[marta-forgestone|Marta Forgestone]].

## Party Interactions

### Session 2

Krispy joined the party alongside [[marta-forgestone|Marta]] for their nighttime run to the circus.

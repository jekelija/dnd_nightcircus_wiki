# Marguerite Voss

## Role

Marguerite Voss is the Chief of Police in Springfield.

## Party Interactions

### Session 3

Voss arrived at the [[the-stone-lion|Stone Lion]] after the party defused a bomb in [[alfred-tennyson|Alfred Tennyson]]'s apartment. She questioned [[poppy-skycleaver|Poppy]] and [[gulfoss|Gulfoss]], but was skeptical that [[robert-frost|Robert Frost]] was actually missing — [[betsy|Betsy]] was the only one who had reported it. When Voss threatened to arrest the party, Poppy tore the "ROBERT" paper, and the group vanished before she could act.

### Session 5

Voss spotted the party crossing campus on their return from the Underground — and saw [[poppy-skycleaver|Poppy]] carrying a body over her shoulder. [[mel|Mel]] cast an illusion to make [[nancy-ann-barbara|Nancy Ann Barbara]] look like a sack of rice while [[robert-frost|Frost]] wafted the smell of donuts across Voss's path, and the party slipped away.

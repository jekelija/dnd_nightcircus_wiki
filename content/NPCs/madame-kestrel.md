# Madame Kestrel — Keeper of Stories

**Ancestry:** Fungril

## Vibe

Warm, wistful, deeply tired.

## Role

Madame Kestrel helps guests choose chairs and encourages them to stay as long as they like.

## Connections

- Quiet ally of [[elias-quill|Elias Quill]]
- Offers refuge to [[tati-kuznetsov|Tati Kuznetsov]]
- Watched—but not controlled—by [[maelin-thorne|Maelin Thorne]]

## Belief

She believes stories are safer than reality—and that this tent is mercy compared to the rest of the circus.

## Act II Stance

She will not stop the party if they choose to act.

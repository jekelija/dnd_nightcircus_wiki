# Alfred Tennyson

## Role

Alfred Tennyson is the curator of the [[the-stone-lion|Stone Lion]], a bookstore in Springfield. He lives in the apartment above it.

## Party Interactions

### Session 3

[[riley-o-shaughnessy|Riley O'Shaughnessy]] warned the party that an explosive had been planted at the Stone Lion. When they arrived, Tennyson buzzed them up to his apartment and let them search his home. They found the bomb and defused it with 18 seconds to spare.

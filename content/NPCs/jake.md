# Jake - the Mourning Husband

**Ancestry:** Katari

## Summary

Jake lost his wife (a sculptor) a few weeks ago; she went to the circus alone, and when he went to meet up with her, she was gone. At the circus he found an ice statue, which is almost a perfect likeness of her. As the ice melts, it forms into a number of her sculptures that she has created over the years before forming anew.

He tells them that since then, he has followed the circus around the country by utilizing a network of fellow circus fans. He waits for a telegram and then takes a train to the circus and waits in this tent for his wife to come back. He is uncertain if the ice sculpture is his wife herself, or somehow a representation of her, or what.

He is currently staying in the Red Dragon Inn.

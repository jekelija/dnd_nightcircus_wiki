# Cornelius Pagewick

## Role

Cornelius Pagewick is the Dean of Arcane Literature at the [[trollbottom-institute|Trollbottom Institute]], and therefore [[robert-frost|Robert Frost]]'s dean.

He is the kind of dean who annotates every book in the restricted section of the [[library|library]] with increasingly passive-aggressive marginalia, and who insists that "arcane literature" properly includes his own three unfinished memoirs. He maintains a running feud with the Divination department over whether prophetic texts count as "literature" or as "cheating."

## Vibe

Territorial about his discipline, easily flattered by anything that sounds like scholarship.

## Party Interactions

### Session 5

[[robert-frost|Frost]] informed Pagewick that he would be taking students on a field trip — Enchanted Archaeology, to [[little-rock|Little Rock]], to view the Cave Poems in the Granite. Pagewick was convinced, and funded the party's train tickets generously enough that they travelled first class.

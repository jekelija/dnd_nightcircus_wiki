# Mr. Glass

## Ancestry

Galapa

## Role

Mr. Glass administered drinks at the [[the-potion-shop|Potion Shop]] with ritual precision, carefully watching reactions. He refused to speak to circus-goers about the circus itself.

He is the one who placed the binding on [[mel|Mel]]'s wrist, and he fought and died in the Underground alongside the [[rotten-rabbit|Rotten Rabbit]] — placing him squarely on the wrong side of whatever the Night Circus is doing to people.

## Vibe

Polite, clinical, unnervingly calm.

> _"You're asking questions meant for daylight. And we're not here for the daylight."_

## Party Interactions

### Session 1

The party drank at the [[the-potion-shop|Potion Shop]] and experienced borrowed memories, including those of a sculptor discussing her latest work. Mr. Glass warned [[candace-cheeks|Cadence]] off her questions. When the ensuing brawl broke out and [[mel|Mel]] stole one of his vials, he caught her by the wrist — and a black band appeared around it.

### Session 5

Mr. Glass burst into the well room with the [[rotten-rabbit|Rotten Rabbit]] and attacked the party, opening by shattering a vial of fear against [[candace-cheeks|Cadence]]. [[mel|Mel]] found she could not attack him at all — her binding flared and blocked her magic entirely. [[candace-cheeks|Cadence]] landed the killing blow, and a loud smash echoed from two rooms over as he fell. Mel picked his pockets and came away with his [[glass-formulary|personal formulary]]. When the Rabbit fell soon after, Mel's binding stopped glowing and her magic returned.

# Elowen Thricebound — Senior Librarian

## Role

Elowen Thricebound is the Senior Librarian of the Restricted Stacks at the [[trollbottom-institute|Trollbottom Institute]]. She is severe in demeanor and rumored to have never left the library and know what book you need before you ask. If you treat her library well, she will treat you well.

## Party Interactions

Candace and Poppy failed to talk their way past her into the restricted section. [[mel|Mel]] later succeeded in getting past, picking the lock while Poppy distracted Thricebound. Gulfoss eventually warned the party via alchemical pad that Thricebound was incoming, prompting their escape.

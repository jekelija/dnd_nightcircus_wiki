# Maelin Thorne — The Quiet Curator

**Ancestry:** Faerie  
**Associated Acts:** None (Logistics & Oversight)

## Role in the Circus

Maelin oversees scheduling, permissions, and curation:

- Who performs when
- Who receives private invitations
- Who is allowed to stay late

She runs no public act.

## Why This Is Dangerous

By removing her from spectacle, Maelin becomes invisible to patrons—and indispensable behind the scenes.

She isn’t testing people.  
She’s filtering them.

## Connections

- Older sister to [[maribel-thorne|Maribel Thorne]]
- Coordinates closely with [[mr-glass|Mr. Glass]]
- Sees [[elias|Elias]] as a liability that should have been removed

## Party Interaction

- Polite, brief, efficient
- Knows where the party shouldn’t be
- Asks unsettlingly specific questions

## Act II Role

Once [[elias|Elias]] speaks up, Maelin becomes the clearest internal antagonist.

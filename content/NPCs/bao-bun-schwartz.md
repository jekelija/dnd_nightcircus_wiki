# Bao Bun Schwartz

## Family

Brother — [[fao-schwartz|Fao Schwartz]]

## Role

Bao Bun Schwartz is the brother of [[fao-schwartz|Fao Schwartz]]. He delves into the dark arts and wrote "A List of Crimes Made Easier by Invisibility."

## Party Interactions

### Session 2

The party found his book in the [[library|library's]] restricted section. It contained a key discovery: a glittery potion was once used to steal memories and drain $1M from a victim's bank account — administered via the victim's tea. The method was later revealed through a golden-thread detection spell. The book's rambling appendix contained a diagram of an eye inside a triangle inside a magnifying glass — a symbol matching the marking [[mel|Mel]] got on her arm.

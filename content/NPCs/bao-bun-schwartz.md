# Bao Bun Schwartz

## Summary

Bao Bun Schwartz is the brother of [[fao-schwartz|Fao Schwartz]]. He wrote "A List of Crimes Made Easier by Invisibility," a book that delves into the dark arts.

## Relevance

His book contained a key finding for the party: a glittery potion was once used to steal memories and drain $1M from a victim's bank account — administered via the victim's tea. The method was later revealed via a golden-thread detection spell. Notably, the book's rambling appendix contained a diagram of an eye inside a triangle inside a magnifying glass — a symbol matching the marking [[mel|Mel]] got on her arm.

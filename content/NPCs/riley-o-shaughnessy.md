# Riley O'Shaughnessy

## Ancestry

Simiah

## Role

Riley O'Shaughnessy is the enigmatic owner of the Night Circus. He dresses as a ringmaster — a red coat against an otherwise black and white outfit — and carries an all-black cane topped with a white marble possum head, very ornate and fancy. He has a tendency to appear where he is not expected, and people seem to walk around him without seeing him, as though space itself bends around his presence.

## Vibe

> _"Secrets have power. And that power diminishes when they are shared, so they are best kept and kept well. Sharing secrets, real secrets, important ones, with even one other person, will change them. Writing them down is worse, because who can tell how many eyes might see them inscribed on paper, no matter how careful you might be with it. So dearies, it's really best to keep your secrets when you have them, for their own good, as well as yours."_

## Party Interactions

### Session 2

[[jake|Jake]] mentioned speaking to Riley about his missing wife. Riley assured Jake that the ice sculpture was not actually his wife, but that the sculptures sometimes form from those who have visited the circus.

### Session 3

Riley appeared outside [[seraphine-vale|Seraphine]]'s tent, invisible to passersby. He told the party he had warned them over and over again, then shared a parable about growing up near a magical forest — his mother told him to stay on the path, but as he grew older he strayed, and the forest felt less magical. He never found another path. He told the party they had already broken things, and forced them to choose: rescue [[robert-frost|Robert]] or save the [[the-stone-lion|Stone Lion]] from a bombing. The party chose the Stone Lion.

### Session 4

[[mel|Mel]] found two scraps of paper on [[robert-frost|Robert Frost]] after the party freed him. One was covered in unintelligible scribbles; the other was a note from Riley reading _"Tear to Return Home."_ The party realized Riley may have been setting them up — the boiling water in the cup puzzle could easily have killed them all.

### Session 5

Studying Riley's note before breaking camp, the party noticed that he dots his i's with little hearts. When [[mel|Mel]] tore the paper, distant laughter answered — and laughter came again the moment the party's hand closed on the vanishing key in the well room, and again when [[mr-glass|Mr. Glass]] and the [[rotten-rabbit|Rotten Rabbit]] came through the door.

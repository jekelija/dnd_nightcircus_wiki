# Tomas "Latch" Campbell — The Compere

**Ancestry:** Infernis  
**Associated Acts:** Bungee Acrobatics

## Vibe

Showman confidence, dark humor, adrenaline addict.

## Role

Latch narrates the acts with gleeful bravado. He revels in danger and the power the circus gives him.

## Connections

- Encouraged by [[mr-glass|Mr. Glass]] to push limits
- Mocked by [[madame-kestrel|Madame Kestrel]] as "loud, but hollow"
- Watched closely by [[isolde-virex|Isolde Virex]]

## Moral Position

- Laughs off ethical questions
- Encourages the crowd to cheer louder
- Urges guests to jump closer, risk more

He is openly intoxicated by the engine’s magic.

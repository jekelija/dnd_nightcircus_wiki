# Elias

## Ancestry

Faun

## Family

Brother — Ezra (former circus worker, missing)

## Role

Elias is a young faun who works backstage at the Night Circus. He and his brother Ezra both joined the circus as backstage janitors after running away from their orphanage. In Chicago, the circus paused for a week, and during that time, Ezra vanished. [[riley-o-shaughnessy|Riley]] told Elias that Ezra left to seek his fortune, but Elias knows his brother would never have left without saying goodbye.

## Vibe

Helpful, forgettable, anxious.

## Party Interactions

### Session 1

The party encountered Elias in the [[the-cave-of-voices|Cave of Voices]]. He was very nervous and told them he was not allowed to speak to circus-goers. He revealed little — only that the Cave of Voices is his favorite spot and that he ran away from an orphanage to join the circus. He fled before they could learn more, and [[gulfoss|Gulfoss]] was apprehended by a guard while trying to follow him.

### Session 2

[[mel|Mel]] slipped into the [[the-cave-of-voices|Cave of Voices]] alone and found Elias again. He revealed that his brother Ezra used to work at the circus before vanishing in Chicago — and that he doesn't buy Riley's story that Ezra simply left.

### Session 5

Elias left a [[granite-note|note]] for the party in the empty field where the Night Circus had been, signed only "E." It contained a fragment of granite pointing them to [[little-rock|Little Rock]], and a plea: _"Please come fast, I'm worried I'm next."_

# The Tween

**Ancestry:** Faun  
**Role:** Backstage, transitions  
**Family:** Brother — Ezra (former circus worker)

- Helpful
- Forgettable
- Anxious

Told them of his brother Ezra. They both joined the circus as backstage janitors after running away from their orphanage. In Chicago, the circus paused for a week, and during that time, Ezra vanished. Riley told him that Ezra left to seek his fortune, but Elias knows that Ezra would never have left without saying goodbye.

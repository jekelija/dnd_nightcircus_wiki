# Alchemical Papers

**Held by:** [[gulfoss|Gulfoss]]

## Description

Real alchemical papers gifted to Gulfoss by [[jake|Jake]] at the [[red-dragon-inn|Red Dragon Inn]] during Session 2. They track the Night Circus's next location, allowing the party to follow its movements. Gulfoss also uses an alchemical pad to send messages to the party (e.g., warning Mel about [[elowen-thricebound|Thricebound]] approaching).

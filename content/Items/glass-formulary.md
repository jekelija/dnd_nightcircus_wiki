# Mr. Glass's Formulary

**Held by:** [[mel|Mel]]

**Location found:** Pickpocketed from [[mr-glass|Mr. Glass]]'s body in the Underground, Session 5

## Description

A small, battered book. [[mel|Mel]] lifted it off [[mr-glass|Mr. Glass]] after [[candace-cheeks|Cadence]] killed him, hoping for the key to the vanished door — she found this instead.

It is Mr. Glass's personal formulary: his working record of the potions he brewed and served at the [[the-potion-shop|Potion Shop]]. Given that Mel's binding is tied to the potion that created it, the formulary may be the closest thing she has to a lead on breaking it.

# Gambler's Biscuits

**Held by:** [[gulfoss|Gulfoss]]

## Description

Magical biscuits carried by Gulfoss that produce unpredictable effects when consumed. Known effects include:

- Knocking a [[circus-guards|circus guard]] unconscious (Session 1)
- Causing [[krystal-nouveau|Krystal Nouveau]] to hear phantom laughter (Session 2)
- Sprouting flowers at [[jake|Jake's]] feet (Session 2)
- Making [[poppy-skycleaver|Poppy]] glow like a beacon for 24 hours (Session 2)
- Causing [[isolde-virex|Isolde Virex]] to hiccough rose petals (Session 2)

# Vial of Glittery Gold Magic

**Held by:** [[mel|Mel]]

## Description

A vial of glittery gold swirling magic-ness, stolen by Mel from [[the-potion-shop|The Potion Shop]] during the party's chaotic escape in Session 1. When Mel grabbed it, the attendant ([[mr-glass|Mr. Glass]]) seized her wrist, and a black band appeared around it.

[[fao-schwartz|Professor Fao Schwartz]] later identified the vial as definitely from the Midnight domain of magic, positively humming with energy. Research into [[bao-bun-schwartz|Bao Bun Schwartz's]] book revealed that a similar glittery potion was once used to steal memories and drain $1M from a victim's bank account.

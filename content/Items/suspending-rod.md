# Suspending Rod

**Held by:** [[gulfoss|Gulfoss]]

## Description

A 36-inch suspending rod won by Gulfoss in a game of King's Bones against [[kenton|Kenton]] and his friend Frenton at the [[red-dragon-inn|Red Dragon Inn]] during Session 2.

In Session 4, Gulfoss used the rod to attempt a vault across the chasm, then to lower the party down into the pit, and finally as a brace to block the inward-opening north door while the party camped.

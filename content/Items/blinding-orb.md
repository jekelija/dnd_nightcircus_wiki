# Blinding Orb

**Held by:** [[mel|Mel]]
**Location found:** The Underground, after destroying a green ooze

## Description

A consumable orb discovered untriggered amid the remains of a massive pulsating green ooze. When activated, all targets within Close range become Vulnerable until they mark HP.

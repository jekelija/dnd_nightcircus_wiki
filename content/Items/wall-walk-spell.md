# Wall Walk Spell

**Held by:** [[poppy-skycleaver|Poppy Skycleaver]]

## Description

A spell token given to Poppy by [[jake|Jake]] at the [[red-dragon-inn|Red Dragon Inn]] during Session 2. It originally belonged to Jake's missing wife [[nancy-ann-barbara|Nancy Ann Barbara]], a sculptor, who would use it to work on her large sculptures. Jake entrusted it to the party.

Used heavily in Session 4: to climb the walls and ceiling above the rat swarm, and repeatedly to get party members across the collapsed-floor chasm.

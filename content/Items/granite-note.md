# The Granite Note

**Held by:** [[mel|Mel]]

**Location found:** The empty field where the Night Circus had been, Session 5

## Description

A note waiting for the party in the grassy field after they stepped through the portal out of the Underground. It reads:

> _"Hope this is enough of a clue for our next location. Please come fast, I'm worried I'm next. —E"_

Folded in with it is a fragment of granite — a **little rock**, pointing the party toward [[little-rock|Little Rock]] as the Night Circus's next stop.

[[mel|Mel]] tore the note the way she had torn [[riley-o-shaughnessy|Riley]]'s. Nothing happened.

It is signed only "E" — [[elias|Elias]], the young faun whose brother Ezra vanished from the circus in Chicago.

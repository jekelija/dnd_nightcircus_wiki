# Candace Cheeks

English lit major. Half dragon, half faerie. Extraordinarily tall, and generally sassy and bold. She is extremely sure of herself, and provides the yin to [[poppy-skycleaver|Poppy Skycleaver's]] yang. Their friendship is formed from a love of English literature and a bond of being just a little bit taller than everyone else.

She idolized the performer [[rotten-rabbit|the Rotten Rabbit]] for as long as she could remember — right up until Session 5, when she met him in the Underground and he tried to kill her. Never meet your heroes.

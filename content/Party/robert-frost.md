# Herr Doctor Professor Robert Frost

Galapa. Professor of Arcane Literature at the [[trollbottom-institute|Trollbottom Institute]], under Dean [[cornelius-pagewick|Cornelius Pagewick]]. He is the object of [[candace-cheeks|Cadence]]'s affections, and [[betsy|Betsy]] — one of his students — appears to be romantically involved with him as well.

Kidnapped and dragged into the Underground, he was found there half-drained of his soul and has travelled with the party ever since. He fights with a great staff and a whip, carries a well-worn copy of _Poems that Make Grown Women Cry_ and a personal journal, and withdraws into his shell when things get bad.

Poppy is permitted to call him Bobby.

## Sanity

In addition to Stress and HP, Frost carries a **Sanity** counter, a measure of how much of himself he has left. It rose from 2 to 12 over the course of Sessions 4 and 5 — coffee, kindness, killing the things that hurt him, and reading his own poetry all help.

## Party Interactions

### Session 2

[[betsy|Betsy]] arrived at the [[red-dragon-inn|Red Dragon Inn]] with alarming news: Robert had been kidnapped.

### Session 3

[[seraphine-vale|Seraphine]]'s card reading revealed the Ten of Wands — Robert carries a heavy burden and seems about to break. [[riley-o-shaughnessy|Riley O'Shaughnessy]] forced the party to choose between rescuing Robert and saving the [[the-stone-lion|Stone Lion]] from a bombing. The party chose the Stone Lion first, then used a slip of paper marked "ROBERT" to transport themselves underground, where they navigated a sprawling maze of rooms, following the distant sound of moaning they believed to be his.

### Session 4

The party found Frost laid out on a stone pedestal, breathing shallowly and moaning, guarded by a puzzle they could not reach past. After solving the cup puzzle — grouping the cups by scent while boiling water rose around them — the wards dropped and [[mel|Mel]] reached him. He was in terrible shape: like [[nancy-ann-barbara|the woman]] they had found earlier, he was losing his soul, though he was far less gone. Mel found two scraps of paper on him, one covered in unintelligible scribbles and one from [[riley-o-shaughnessy|Riley]] reading _"Tear to Return Home."_ His Sanity counter began at 2, rising to 3 when he pulled into his shell during camp.

### Session 5

Frost came out of his shell swinging in the fight against [[mr-glass|Mr. Glass]] and the [[rotten-rabbit|Rotten Rabbit]], and recovered steadily through the session — coffee from Mel, Poppy checking on him, the Rabbit's death, and time with Cadence, his journal, Poppy, and Mel brought his Sanity from 3 to 12, with a 2-point loss along the way when his Stress maxed out during the portal puzzle. He smashed the lute Mel looted for Cadence, wafted the smell of donuts to distract [[marguerite-voss|Chief Voss]] during the party's escape across campus, and convinced Dean [[cornelius-pagewick|Cornelius Pagewick]] to fund a first-class "Enchanted Archaeology field trip" to [[little-rock|Little Rock]]. He recovered his staff, whip, journal, and copy of _Poems that Make Grown Women Cry_ from his office.

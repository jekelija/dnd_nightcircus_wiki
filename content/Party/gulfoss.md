# Gulfoss

A student at Trollbottom's culinary school. An obsessive gambler, he likes to make "gambler's biscuits"; biscuits that he packs with random magic to just "see what happens". Despite this dark streak, Gulfoss has a kind heart and has been known to sneak extra food to [[poppy-skycleaver|Poppy]], as the school mealhall's portions are not enough for the gentle giant.

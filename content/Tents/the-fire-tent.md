# 🔥 The Fire Tent

**Associated NPC:** [[isolde-virex|Isolde Virex]]

Heat blooms instantly. Black and white flames spiral into deliberate forms.

A fire eater exhales living embers. A sculptor pulls blazing figures from open flame—animals, faces, impossible geometry—holding them steady with bare hands.

The fire responds to movement, emotion, applause.

## Notable Feature

**The Lantern Procession** originates here.

## Interaction

- Catch a fire sculpture after naming what it should be
- Near the end, [[isolde-virex|Isolde Virex]] may ask a character to release the first lantern

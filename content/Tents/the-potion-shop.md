# 🧪 The Potion Shop

**Associated NPC:** [[mr-glass|Mr. Glass]]

Shelves curve around the tent, packed with softly glowing jars. Each drink smells achingly familiar: summer rain, baked bread, clean linen, pine smoke.

As you sip, you are somewhere else—lost in memory so vivid it almost hurts to return.

Some patrons linger too long.

## Sign Displayed

> _To choose a drink, a spell, a voice,_  
> _What factors into your choice?_  
> _To choose many is no sin,_  
> _But be wary of those that stand a twin._

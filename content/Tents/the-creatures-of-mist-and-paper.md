# 📜 Creatures of Mist and Paper

**Associated NPC:** [[tati-kuznetsov|Tati Kuznetsov]]

Mist clings low to the ground, curling around delicate figures folded from paper and lacquered thin enough to breathe.

They move carefully. Wings flutter. Joints crease and uncrease.

When the fog thickens, some shapes blur—as if the paper remembers other forms.

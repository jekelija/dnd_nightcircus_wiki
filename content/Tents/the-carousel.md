# 🎠 The Carousel

<!-- **Associated NPC:** [[maribel-thorne|Maribel Thorne]]-->

The carousel waits in stillness, its carved beasts frozen in poised elegance—until a rider swings a leg over the saddle.

At once, the animal beneath you inhales.

Wood softens into muscle. Paint fractures into scales or fur. The platform vanishes beneath pounding hooves or coiling wings. A warhorse surges forward with breath hot against your hands; a lion rolls its shoulders and growls low in its chest; a dragon’s wings unfurl just enough to make your stomach drop.

The ride never leaves the carousel’s circle, yet every sense insists you are charging across battlefields, savannahs, and open sky.

When the music ends, the creatures slow, exhale, and become art once more—leaving the echo of motion thrumming in your bones.

# 🔮 The Fortune Teller

**Associated NPC:** [[seraphine-vale|Seraphine Vale]]

The tent is small and warm, heavy with incense that smells of crushed herbs and old paper.

A low table waits at the center, scattered with cards, bones, threads, and objects you don’t immediately recognize as tools.

When the fortune teller looks at you, it feels less like being seen—and more like being remembered.

Her voice is calm. Certain. Entirely unconcerned with whether you like what she has to say.

# 🪢 The Hanged Man

**Associated NPC:** [[thomas-campbell|Tomas "Latch" Campbell]]

You step into darkness and instinctively look up.

Far above, bodies twist and fall—and stop short. Silks snap taut. Chairs swing like pendulums. Glass globes rotate slowly with figures curled impossibly inside.

A compere plummets past you headfirst, arrested inches from the ground by a bungee line that hums like a plucked string.

There is no net. No visible safety.

Only the constant whisper of motion, and the unsettling realization that the performers never miss, never slip, never tire.

# ❄️ The Ice Garden

Cold rushes over you as the tent opens into a garden far larger than its footprint should allow.

Ice arches overhead like frozen vines. Sculptures bloom and collapse in slow, deliberate stages. Meltwater runs in narrow channels through the floor as one figure softens and slumps while another rises elsewhere—continuing a story told entirely in frost and motion.

The chill never hurts. It only lingers.

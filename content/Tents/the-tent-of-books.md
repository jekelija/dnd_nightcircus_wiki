# 📚 The Tent of Books

**Associated NPC:** [[madame-kestrel|Madame Kestrel]]

The tent holds only chairs and lamplight.

When you sit and close your eyes, a story unfolds in perfect clarity—voices, emotions, moments lived.

When you stand, it fades.

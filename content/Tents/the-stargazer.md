# ✨ The Stargazer

**Associated NPC:** [[orin-bellweather|Orin Bellweather]]

You are seated into a gently climbing track. The car tilts backward until the sky fills your vision.

Stars blaze overhead—impossibly close. Constellations rearrange themselves as you drift upward. With a thought, you can draw a star nearer and watch it swell into fire, dust, motion.

It is breathtaking. And faintly disorienting.

## Seating

Seats are arranged in groups of three.

Who sits next to whom?

If seated beside a stranger, roll **Presence**.

## Presence Roll

It is [[orin-bellweather|Orin Bellweather]] himself. He is furtive—aware he is not meant to speak.

**Success**

- “Something is off. I couldn’t tell you what, but the owner gives me the heeby-jeebies.”
- He speaks of **Riley O’Shaughnessy**, who recruited him from magician school.
- “My magic is amplified here. The results are… breathtaking.”

The ride reminds both of you how small—and how vast—the universe is.

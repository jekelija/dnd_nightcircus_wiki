# ☁️ The Cloud Maze

**Associated NPC:** [[calder-bloom|Calder Bloom]]

A sign reads:

> **THE CLOUD MAZE**  
> An Excursion in Dimension  
> There Is No Beginning  
> There Is No End  
> Enter Where You Please  
> Leave When You Wish  
> Have No Fear of Falling

The floor is soft and buoyant—clouds compressing and rebounding beneath your feet. Paths split and rejoin, climbing gradually upward. Step too far and you fall harmlessly out, drifting down to solid ground below.

Laughter echoes strangely here.

The higher you climb, the stronger the urge becomes to leap.

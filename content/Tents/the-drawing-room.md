# ✏️ The Drawing Room

**Associated NPC:** [[tati-kuznetsov|Tati Kuznetsov]]

Blackboards line every surface. Chalk lies in buckets at your feet.

Guests sketch idly—until drawings begin to shift. Chalk birds flutter. Landscapes spill across the floor. A hastily drawn monster blinks in surprise at its own existence.

Nothing lasts forever. Eventually, everything smudges and fades.

## Interaction

Depending on what is drawn, trigger an **Action Roll** to interact with it.

This allows conversation with [[tati-kuznetsov|Tati Kuznetsov]].

## Fear Payoff — _Imagination Has Consequences_

Something the character once drew appears again later—changed.

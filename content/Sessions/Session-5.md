## Summary

### Hearts Over the I's

Before they break camp, the party takes another look at [[riley-o-shaughnessy|Riley O'Shaughnessy]]'s note. Riley, it turns out, dots his i's with little hearts. The detail sits uneasily alongside everything else they know about him.

[[poppy-skycleaver|Poppy]] has finally stopped glowing. [[mel|Mel]] brews coffee for the group, and the simple normalcy of it steadies [[robert-frost|Robert Frost]] — his Sanity ticks up to 4.

Poppy heaves [[nancy-ann-barbara|the soulless woman]] over her shoulder, the party closes ranks, and Mel rips the paper.

### The Well

Distant laughter answers the tear. A new door appears in the wall of the room — a glowing keyhole ringed in glowing blue edges. Stone grinds somewhere beneath the floor, and a well rises up in front of them, brimming with water. A little distance away, two vessels appear.

One vessel is a glass case holding five large mugs. Around the rim of the well sit five smaller mugs. The other vessel is a glass box, and inside it is a key.

Poppy grabs one of the small cups, and a countdown timer starts: 3:30. The party races to ferry water from the well into the vessels, filling them until the mugs and the key come free. They manage it — but the instant they close a hand around the key, it vanishes, and the laughter returns. The large mugs do not come away with them either.

### Mr. Glass and the Rotten Rabbit

The door bursts open. Out come [[mr-glass|Mr. Glass]] — the potion shop attendant who put the binding on Mel back in Session 1 — and the [[rotten-rabbit|Rotten Rabbit]]. They shut the door behind them, and the keyhole disappears.

[[candace-cheeks|Cadence]] recognizes the Rabbit instantly, and explains to the rest of the party who he is: not some anonymous circus goon in a mask, but the Rotten Rabbit, a genuine icon — a performer she has idolized for years.

Glass opens by hurling a glass vial at Cadence. It shatters against her and floods her with fear. Frost pops out of his shell and starts swinging his staff. [[gulfoss|Gulfoss]] shoots Glass — the shot lands, but does no apparent damage. Mel lines up an attack and simply cannot execute it: her binding flares, and no magic passes through her at all.

Up close, the Rotten Rabbit's mask has slipped a little.

The party tucks into the fight — all except Cadence, who cannot bring herself to raise a hand against him. That lasts exactly as long as it takes for the Rabbit to make plain that he intends to kill them all. The luster comes off fast. Never meet your heroes, kids.

Poppy and Cadence both take a potion that leaves them nauseated. Gulfoss offers his support from the back: _"Kick his ass, Mr. Turtle Man!"_

Cadence lands the final blow on Mr. Glass. As he falls, a loud smash echoes from two rooms over. Mel picks his pockets and comes away with a small, battered book — but no key.

Poppy checks in on Frost, asking how he's holding up, and the plain fact of being cared for brings his Sanity to 5. Mel then takes the Rotten Rabbit down entirely, and Frost's Sanity leaps to 8. Mel loots a lute off the body and hands it to Cadence; Frost smashes it. In the same moment, Mel's binding stops glowing, and her magic comes back.

### The Waiting Room

Gulfoss pushes through into the second room, which looks unlike anything else in the maze. It is still fifty feet square, but it resembles a waiting room — chairs lined along the side walls, and instead of doors, an inactive portal on the opposite wall.

In the middle of the room lie large pieces of stone debris, as though a giant had smashed a huge stone platter against the floor. In front of the debris is a recessed circle in the ground, and in front of that, a button. Markings and glyphs cover the broken platter, but the party cannot quite make them out.

A plaque on the button reads:

> _"The first hint is free, after that you have to pay the piper."_

Poppy starts sorting through the pieces looking for edges, and the moment she does, a timer starts — the overall clock on how long the party has to finish the puzzle. Gulfoss presses the button, which starts a second, much shorter timer: how long they get to look at the "correct" image before it disappears again.

The first hint is free. After that, the piper wants paying, and the price is blood — both [[mel|Mel]] and [[poppy-skycleaver|Poppy]] take electrical damage buying further looks at the image. The party fails several times before the pieces settle. Somewhere in the grind, [[robert-frost|Frost]]'s Stress maxes out and he loses 2 Sanity, dropping from 8 to 6.

Then the platter comes together, and the portal opens onto what looks like a grassy field.

### A Little Rock

The party steps through and finds itself standing in the field where the Night Circus used to be. The circus is gone.

Waiting for them is a note:

> _"Hope this is enough of a clue for our next location. Please come fast, I'm worried I'm next. —E"_

Folded in with it is a fragment of granite. A **little rock**, if you will.

The "E" is [[elias|Elias]] — the anxious young faun who has been feeding them scraps since the first night, and whose brother Ezra already vanished from the circus without a trace.

Mel tears the note. Nothing happens.

### Back to School

The gang heads back toward the [[trollbottom-institute|Trollbottom Institute]]. On the way, Mel examines the book she lifted off Mr. Glass: it is his personal formulary, a working record of his potions.

[[marguerite-voss|Marguerite Voss]] spots them from across the way — and sees Poppy carrying a body. Mel casts an illusion to make [[nancy-ann-barbara|Nancy Ann Barbara]] look like a sack of rice, Frost wafts the smell of donuts across Voss's path to distract her, and the party slips away.

They stash Nancy Ann Barbara in Poppy's dorm and take Frost to the school nurse. Gulfoss writes to [[jake|Jake]] on the [[alchemical-papers|alchemical papers]] to tell him they have NABBED N.A.B., and to meet them in Little Rock.

A look at the timetables confirms the trip: two three-hour trains to reach Little Rock.

### Errands

The party splits up to prepare.

**Mel** visits [[fao-schwartz|Professor Doctor Doctor Fao Schwartz]]. He has no real answers about her binding — he explains that breaking it would be tied to the potion itself, and that what it takes depends entirely on what the binding is bound _to_. He looks at it and tells her, plainly, that it is very dark. Mel convinces him that she is "ok." He suggests necromancy may hold the answer — and then adds, pointedly, that Trollbottom has no professors of necromancy, it being a dark art and certainly not something practiced at school.

**Cadence** takes Frost to the nurse, who does some minor healing. [[krispy-kripke|Krispy Kripke]] rolls in while they are there and is visibly shocked to see Frost alive and upright. Kripke suggests that a poetry reading might do him some good.

**Poppy** recruits [[marta-forgestone|Professor Marta Forgestone]] to repair her armor.

**Frost** goes to see [[cornelius-pagewick|Cornelius Pagewick]], Dean of Arcane Literature, and informs him that the group will be going on a field trip: Enchanted Archaeology, to Little Rock, to view the Cave Poems in the Granite. He talks Pagewick into funding the train tickets — generously enough that the party rides first class.

**Frost and Cadence** stop by his office. Cadence lays on the moves, and Frost's Sanity climbs to 7. He collects his book _Poems that Make Grown Women Cry_, his personal journal — paging through it recovers him further, to 9 — his great staff, and his whip, the sight of which causes Cadence to entirely lose her cool.

**Poppy** pulls Herr Doctor Professor Robert Frost aside for a talk (she can call him Bobby), which gains him 2 more Sanity, to 11. **Mel** then has a good moment preparing alongside him, bringing him to 12.

Poppy goes to work on the [[magic-cattle-prod|Magic Cattle Prod]] for Mel.

## Starting Location

[[the-underground|The Underground]] — a dead-end room deep in the maze, with rats piled against the door.

## Party Members

- [[candace-cheeks|Candace Cheeks]]
- [[gulfoss|Gulfoss]]
- [[mel|Mel]]
- [[poppy-skycleaver|Poppy Skycleaver]]

## Notable NPCs

- [[robert-frost|Robert Frost]]
- [[riley-o-shaughnessy|Riley O'Shaughnessy]] (via a note found on Frost)

## Items Found

- [[blinding-orb|Blinding Orb]] (untriggered; found after destroying the ooze)

## Summary

### The Rats

The session picks up with the party cornered in a dead-end room, rats piled against the only door. The group uses [[wall-walk-spell|Wall Walk]] to climb high up the walls and ceiling. [[poppy-skycleaver|Poppy]] smashes the door open with her warhammer from above, and rats flood into the room below. [[mel|Mel]] casts an illusion to distract them, and the party scrambles out over the swarm. The escape is not clean — Poppy takes mild poisoning from the rats, while [[candace-cheeks|Cadence]] and [[gulfoss|Gulfoss]] are both badly poisoned. Mel administers antidotes to both, steadying them enough to keep moving.

### Retracing Steps

The party backtracks through familiar territory, hunting for [[robert-frost|Robert Frost]]'s trail. Heading west, Mel triggers a rune on the wall and takes a blast of energy. They press north through an empty room, then north again — a door opens onto a dead end. [[gulfoss|Gulfoss]] uses his gifted tracker ability and determines that their quarry was never in this part of the maze. Frost's trail leads to the northeast.

They double back south, skirting the rune this time, and eventually pass through the silent room — the oppressive quiet swallowing all sound once more — before pushing north into uncharted territory.

### The Chasm

The party enters a room where the floor has caved in almost entirely. Only narrow five-foot ledges remain on either side, with a jagged pit stretching thirty to forty feet deep between them. On the far ledge, two sets of empty manacles hang from heavy chains bolted to iron rings in the wall. A door leads onward beyond them.

Crossing proves agonizing. [[gulfoss|Gulfoss]] extends his [[suspending-rod|Suspending Rod]] and attempts to vault across, but fails — though he manages to land back on the ledge rather than in the pit. He tries again with a rope, but the rope vanishes mid-crossing. [[poppy-skycleaver|Poppy]] hugs [[mel|Mel]] and casts [[wall-walk-spell|Wall Walk]] on her; Mel succeeds in walking across the wall to the far side. Gulfoss tries yet again and fails spectacularly. [[candace-cheeks|Cadence]] flies to the rod suspended in midair, and Gulfoss deactivates it so she can glide to the far ledge. But the moment Poppy casts Wall Walk on Gulfoss, everyone is inexplicably transported back to the starting ledge.

Frustrated, they try a different approach. Gulfoss uses his rod to lower himself and the others down into the chasm. From the pit floor, Cadence flies up to the far ledge. Poppy wall-walks across the chasm wall. Eventually, through sheer stubbornness and creative teamwork, the entire party makes it to the other side.

### The Soulless Woman

Past the chasm, the party hears howling that cuts off abruptly the moment they notice it. They press east and find a woman's body lying on the stone floor. Her eyes are open, dried blood rings her wrists, and her pupils do not dilate — but she has a pulse.

[[poppy-skycleaver|Poppy]] recognizes her. She looks like the woman from the ice sculpture in the [[the-ice-garden|Ice Garden]] — [[jake|Jake]]'s missing wife.

[[mel|Mel]] examines her and delivers a grim diagnosis: the woman has no soul. Whatever happened to her, her body persists, but the person inside is gone. Poppy hoists the woman over her shoulder, and the party presses on.

### The Ooze

The next room contains an overwhelmingly large, pulsating green ooze that fills the space. [[robert-frost|Robert Frost]]'s moaning echoes from somewhere to the north. The ooze attacks — [[gulfoss|Gulfoss]] takes a brutal hit — but the party rallies and destroys it. In the aftermath, they discover a [[blinding-orb|Blinding Orb]], untriggered, which Mel tucks into her bag.

They continue north through an unremarkable room. Poppy reminds Mel to tread carefully, and sure enough the floor crumbles beneath Mel's feet as she moves — though she escapes unharmed.

### The Cup Puzzle

The party heads east into a wide room with doors to the north and east. The moment they enter, heavy iron grates slam shut over every exit. The engravings on the grates depict people dying and screaming. Six stone pedestals stand roughly four feet high throughout the room. On one pedestal lies [[robert-frost|Robert Frost]], breathing shallowly, still moaning. On another sit ten drink containers of varying shapes, sizes, and colors. The remaining four pedestals are empty.

[[mel|Mel]] reaches over to waft smelling salts under Frost's nose, but an invisible force repels her hand. [[gulfoss|Gulfoss]] tries sprinkling gambler's biscuits and flowers — also repelled. They cannot reach Frost directly.

Mel encourages the party to examine the cups. Each container is numbered one through eleven — with ten total. The puzzle, they reason, involves placing the cups on the four empty pedestals in the correct groupings. They begin experimenting.

Cups 1, 3, 4, and 8 — the walls rumble and shake, but nothing else happens. They swap 8 for 6 — more rumbling, and this time boiling water begins seeping through unseen openings in the floor. Another combination brings the water to ankle height. Another raises it to eight inches. The room grows dangerously hot.

Gulfoss wafts cup #11 and catches a whiff of vanilla — something from his kitchen. He takes an impulsive sip and is immediately poisoned. Mel's alchemy training confirms her suspicion that all of the cups are dangerous to drink, but she wafts #9 and recognizes the scent of her home by the ocean. Cup #7 smells roughly the same.

The smell is the key. The party shifts strategy and begins grouping cups by scent rather than by number or appearance: cups 4, 8, 6, and 10 on one pedestal; 5 and 11 on another; 1, 7, and 9 on a third; 3 and 2 on the last. When the final cup is placed, all four pedestals sink into the floor with a deep grinding sound. The boiling water recedes. There is a sharp pop, and Robert Frost's moaning becomes suddenly, clearly audible. The iron grates rise.

### Robert Frost

[[mel|Mel]] rushes to [[robert-frost|Robert Frost]]. He is in terrible shape. She ascertains that he has been losing his soul — the same affliction as the woman they found — but is less far gone. The party realizes with a chill that [[riley-o-shaughnessy|Riley O'Shaughnessy]] may have been setting them up. The boiling water could easily have killed them all.

Mel finds two scraps of paper on Frost's person. One is covered in unintelligible scribbles. The other is a note from Riley that reads: _"Tear to Return Home."_ Mel suggests they take Frost back to the school.

Frost now carries a Sanity counter in addition to stress and HP. It sits at 2.

### Camp

The party decides to rest where they are. [[gulfoss|Gulfoss]] blocks the north door — which opens inward — with his [[suspending-rod|Suspending Rod]] and ties a rope from the rod to the west door, which opens outward. [[poppy-skycleaver|Poppy]] lies down in front of the east door to block it with her body. They set up a watch rotation.

As the party settles in, [[robert-frost|Robert Frost]] pulls into his shell unconsciously — and his Sanity rises to 3. The party takes a long rest.

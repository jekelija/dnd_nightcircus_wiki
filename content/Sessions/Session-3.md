## Summary

### The Fortune Teller

The session begins in [[seraphine-vale|Seraphine]]'s tent. Gulfoss attempts to offer her a gambler's biscuit, but Poppy intercepts him before he can. Seraphine lays out a reading for Poppy:

| Card Drawn | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Ten of Swords | — |
| The Rabbit: Seven of Cups | Many paths lie ahead; beware choosing the correct cup |
| The Cat (warning/caution): King of Swords | Beware of bald men, crows sitting at your shoulder, and unnatural chairs |
| Seven of Wands | One of the party will have to make a decision alone |
| The Fall: Knight of Pentacles | Someone may find themselves transported against their will |
| Deep into the Underground: Knight of Wands | Opposing knights; forces of good vs. forces of evil — the party must choose a side |
| The World: Five of Swords | There are only three swords left; one of the party may perish |

The reading paints a journey fraught with peril. The cards vanish as Seraphine finishes — though the whole affair feels lackluster and uninspiring with Poppy lighting up the tent like Christmas. Seraphine warns the party that they will not find what they are looking for, but might find what they are NOT looking for.

### Mel's Orb

Mel attempts to sneakily share her orb with Seraphine, but the entire party watches her pass it over. Seraphine takes an interest in it immediately — she hears a troubled spirit within and warns Mel not to take what it says at face value, as it may lie without "more work." She advises that if Mel spends time with the orb during long rests, she will get better results. When Seraphine hands it back, Mel peers into the orb and senses that the spirit inside may be sentient.

Mel asks the orb if she is going to die. The response:

> _"The Candle Knows its Wax will pool but burns brightest in its final hours."_

### Reading for Mel

Cheeks asks whether the cards could find a missing person. Seraphine agrees to read for Mel, who confesses that her mother is not well. Mel reveals she is from the Wildborne clan, and her mother is a Mage of Primal Origin.

| Card Drawn | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Knight of Cups | Someone has been carried away |
| Seven of Cups | Beware of the cups — cups will be offered, carrying both reward and penalty |
| Ten of Wands | [[robert-frost|Robert]] carries a heavy burden; he seems about to break — the party must get to him quickly |

Seraphine mentions that she and [[isolde-virex|Isolde]] have been friends since school and joined the circus around the same time. They keep to themselves, away from the other performers, because they have "too many secrets." Poppy asks if many people go missing from the circus and is promptly kicked out of the tent.

### The Ringmaster

As Poppy exits, she spots a man standing in front of the tent. People walk around him, but none of them seem to see him — almost as if space itself bends around his presence. The rest of the party emerges and sees him too. None of them recognize him.

He is dressed as a ringmaster, wearing a red coat against an otherwise black and white outfit. Mel notices he carries an all-black cane topped with a white marble possum head — very ornate and fancy. The man introduces himself as [[riley-o-shaughnessy|Riley O'Shaughnessy]], and tells the party he has warned them over and over again.

Riley shares a parable: as a child, he grew up near a beautiful, magical forest, and his mother told him to stay on the path. As he grew older, he strayed from it, and the forest felt less magical. He never found another path. He tells the party that they have already broken things, and now they face a choice — rescue [[robert-frost|Robert]], or save the [[the-stone-lion|Stone Lion]]. The implication is grim: an explosive has been planted at the Stone Lion, and everyone inside will be killed, including the owner who lives above the bookstore.

Mel consults her orb — "Frost or Lion" — and announces: "I think we go with the Lion."

### The Stone Lion

Poppy tears the slip of paper with the Stone Lion written on it, and the party is immediately transported to the bookstore. Lights glow upstairs in the curator's apartment. [[alfred-tennyson|Alfred Tennyson]] buzzes them up, and they explain the situation. A frantic search of his home reveals a bomb. With 18 seconds to spare, the party cuts the correct wire. The bomb does not go off. Shortly after, the authorities arrive on scene.

[[marguerite-voss|Marguerite Voss]], the Chief of Police, speaks with Poppy and Gulfoss while Mel and Cadence quietly steal five books from Tennyson's shelves. The police are skeptical that [[robert-frost|Robert]] is actually missing, since only [[betsy|Betsy]] reported it. As Voss threatens to arrest the party, Poppy tears the "ROBERT" paper and the group vanishes — reappearing in a dark, dank stone room.

### The Underground

The room would be dark and dank without Poppy's aggressive glow. It stretches roughly 50 feet square, hewn from rock with natural stone formations jutting from the walls. There is no door behind them, but at least one lies straight ahead. Gulfoss sniffs for the decay of rotting flesh but only smells damp. Mel reaches out with her senses and detects no magical presence — no creatures and no objects. The party finds doors on the North and West walls.

**Room 1 (Start):** The entry room. Doors lead North and West.

**Room 2:** Mel opens the North door into a room of the same size, equally empty, with exits North and East. A huge bat swoops past Cadence's face and terrifies her.

**Room 3:** Mel pushes through the North door. The interstitial walls begin closing in — Cadence takes damage, and Poppy sticks close to protect her. Doors lead North and East.

**Room 4:** Mel takes the East door. Another 50-foot square room opens up, and in the corner sits a [[featherbone|Featherbone]] that controls falling speed. Doors lead East, West, and North.

**Room 5:** Mel chooses East.

**Room 6:** The party doubles back West into the room they just left. Cadence and Gulfoss trigger a trap — rusted iron spikes swing down from the ceiling and injure them both.

**Room 7:** They head North. A moan echoes from somewhere far in the distance. A single door leads North.

**Room 8:** Pressing North, the party enters a room filled with a low, loud thumping — like the sound of marching, emanating from the Northeast. Gulfoss scouts around the corner and a massive stone-and-earth Golem takes a swing at him. The towering creature spots Poppy roughly 25 feet away. A fight breaks out; Cadence casts a fog spell, and the party scampers through the North door.

**Room 9:** Mel steps into the next room. Her magic-sensing ability flares wildly — and she vanishes.

**Room 10:** The remaining party heads West and hears a grinding noise. The room spins; they fall to the ground, and when they recover, the only exit leads South.

**Room 11:** Gulfoss takes the lead for the next stretch of rooms.

**Room 12:** Gulfoss discovers a throwing blade embedded in the stone, Arthurian-style. From the West, they hear what sounds like Mel. Exits lead East and North.

**Room 13:** They head North. The maze falls dead silent. Cadence and Gulfoss grow anxious — they can no longer hear Mel. Cadence sings a calming song to steady the party's nerves.

**Room 14:** They turn West. Sound returns to normal. From the North comes moaning — possibly Robert — and from the South, the unmistakable sound of Mel shouting "Hell yeah."

**Room 15:** The party heads South toward the sound of Mel. In the center of the room hovers a minor Chaos Elemental, howling like a windstorm. It attacks Cadence and Poppy, warping the smooth walls into jagged rock that slashes Cadence. As Gulfoss moves far enough from the Elemental, he can hear Mel clearly. He calls out "reveal yourself," and an arm thrusts through the wall. Gulfoss grabs it and holds on, pulling Mel bodily out of the Astral Plane — she takes minor injuries from the extraction. Mel swipes at the Elemental as she staggers past; Gulfoss takes a swing of his own. A chaotic fight ensues until Mel drags herself clear and Poppy lands the killing blow. The party retreats to the previous room to regroup.

**Room 16 (& 14):** Cadence delivers a rallying speech to lift the party's spirits.

**Room 17:** They push North. The moaning grows softer.

**Room 18:** They continue North. The moaning persists, faintly.

**Room 19:** North again. Something brushes past Poppy, but none of them pay it any mind — they stay focused on tracking the moaning, though they cannot pinpoint its direction. Exits lead North, West, and South. Gulfoss scans the room and sees nothing. Mel discovers a small flying creature, which promptly bites her on the nose.

**Room 20:** They head West. A glyph on the wall activates and catches Poppy with a burst of energy. The moaning falls silent.

**Room 21:** North. Mel takes the lead and casts minor illusion, growing herself one size larger.

**Room 22:** They pass back through the previous room.

**Room 23:** The party returns to the creature room. The moaning remains absent.

**Room 24:** They head North into a room of dripping water and faint scampering. Creeping toward the East exit, they spot two giant rats. The rats lunge at Mel's illusion — one sees through it and turns on the real Mel. Poppy cuts down one rat; Mel dispatches the other. Then a flood of rats pours around the corner.

**Room 25 (Dead End):** They flee East into a dead end. Rats pile against the door behind them. Mel and Cadence find a pair of short swords in the room. The moaning has stopped entirely. Cornered, Mel proposes slipping into the Astral Plane to bypass the rats entirely.

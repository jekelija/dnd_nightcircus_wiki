## 📜 Summary

The students initially decide to talk to their professors in order to get more information about the vial that [[mel|Mel]] stole. They start with her alchemy professor, Professor Doctor Doctor Fao Schwartz, who wrote the potions book "Stirring Potions for Beginners". He tells them the vial is definitely from the Midnight domain of magic, and positively humming with energy. With some trepidation, he tells them to visit the library and check out a book by his brother, Bao Bun Schwartz, called "A List of Crimes Made Easier by Invisibility." He tells them that he went to the Night Circus and found it delightful, even conjuring a paper tiger on the blackboard. He pointed the party toward Professor Krystal Nouveau for questions about Transmutation, as he had no knowledge of any potions that would turn someone into an ice sculpture. Mel and Gulfoss visited Madame Nouveau, where Gulfoss opened with a gambler's biscuit — causing her to hear phantom laughter. Despite the rocky start, she answered their questions about transmuting a living person into an ice sculpture: it's possible, but permanent, dangerous, and roughly a 50/50 shot even for her. She warned the result couldn't be reversed cleanly. She said she had once seen it at a funeral, and would be easier to do on someone already dead. On her way out, she reminded Mel to study Animal Transmutations Volume III. Meanwhile, Poppy and Cadence headed to the library, where they encountered:

Bramwell Quill, the rigid junior librarian (do not call him Bram)
Eloin Thricebound, the severe head librarian with a rumored supernatural ability to produce any needed key and know what book you need before you ask.
Cadence and Poppy failed to talk their way into the restricted section — but Mel succeeded, and while Poppy talked to Thricebound about finding her a book, Mel picked the lock.

### The Library Minigame

The party navigated the restricted section covertly. Key findings:

List of Crimes: A glittery potion was used to steal memories and drain $1M from a victim's bank account — the method was later revealed via a golden-thread detection spell. The potion was administered in the victim's tea. Notably, the book had a rambling appendix that talked about how they found the criminals in the book. A diagram in that appendix has an eye inside a triangle inside a magnifying glass; a symbol that matches the marking Mel got on her arm.

Curses Through the Ages: Successfully stolen by Cadence; later revealed to contain a section on breaking binding curses — massively complex, requiring obscure ingredients. No leads on tattoos, potions, or memory magic.
Other books (Field Guide, Divination of the Arcane, Things Better Left for the Dead, Time Travel Apocalypse Now and Later) yielded nothing actionable and were left at the lbirary.

Gulfoss warned Mel via alchemical pad: "Thricebound inbound." The team escaped. Poppy, panicking, ate one of Gulfoss' gambler's biscuits and is spending the next 24 hours glowing like a beacon.

### The Red Dragon Inn

The gang met Kenton at the inn. Gulfoss played King's Bones against Kenton and his friend Frenton and walked away with extra gold and a suspending rod (36").
The party found [[jake|Jake]], fed him a gambler's biscuit (flowers sprouted at his feet), and gathered intel:

- Circus guards rotate when the circus moves locations
- The circus appears deserted and unguarded during the day
- Jake gifted Gulfoss alchemical papers (real ones) tracking the Night Circus's next location
- Jake gave Poppy a Wall Walk Spell token belonging to his missing wife that she would use to work on her large sculptures
- Jake spoke to [[riley-o-shaugnessy|Riley]], who assured him the sculpture was not actually his wife, but that the ice sculptures sometimes form from those who have visited; a sculpture of Jake has never shown up though

Jake bid them farewell, and said he probably would not go back to the circus tonight.

As they walked out of the inn, Betsy arrived with alarming news: Professor Robert Frost (Arcane Lit — and apparently Cadence's crush and Betsy's implied affair) had been kidnapped.

### Preparations & Allies

The party visited Professor Marta Forgestone (Dean of Enchanted Smithery), who helped construct an arcane combat wheelchair for another professor, Krispy Kripke. Poppy shared some of what they'd run into so far at the circus, and both Marta and Krispy joined the party for the circus run.

### At the Night Circus

[[the-stargazer|Stargazer Tent]]: The group rode mining carts through a stunning constellation experience, guided by Orin Bellweather.
[[the-cave-of-voices|Cave of Voices]]: Mel slipped in alone and found [[elias|Elias]] again. He revealed his brother Ezra used to work at the circus before vanishing in Chicago — and he doesn't buy Riley's story that Ezra simply left. Listening in the wind, Mel caught a fragment: a sculptor speaking about her work.
[[the-fire-tent|Fire Tent]]: The party met [[isolde-virex|Isolde Virex]], a severe Drakona fire sculptor. She and Cadence combined their breath to create a glass sculpture (a butt plug — Mel kept it). Gulfoss fed Isolde a gambler's biscuit; she began hiccoughing rose petals and warmed up considerably, sharing that she's been with the circus 20 years, seems wistful about it, and sleeps in Car #3 of the train — sharing a compartment with Seraphine, the fortune teller. More important staff ride in the rear cars.

Marta and Crispy broke off for food. The rest of the party headed to see the fortune teller...
